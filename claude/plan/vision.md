# Product vision
Status: In review

## One line
A friendly companion that shows you how much you swipe through short videos, so you can decide for yourself what to do about it.

## Problem
Short-form video feeds are built for endless swiping. People lose track of how much they watch.
Existing tools mostly measure screen time or block apps, which feels punishing and gets uninstalled.

## Who it's for
- Students and young professionals who feel they "lose hours" to reels.
- People who want awareness, not parental-control style blocking.

## What makes it different
- **Awareness-first:** we count swipes and make the number feel real, but never block by default.
- **Opt-in control:** swipe budgets and focus mode exist, but only if the user turns them on.
- **Emotional hook:** a virtual pet or plant that reflects your habits.
- **Social, but private:** an optional accountability buddy instead of public leaderboards by default.

## Feature set
| Feature | Default | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Swipe counter (core) | On | 1 |
| Make it real (distance, time, equivalents) | On | 1 |
| Insights (daily and weekly) | On | 1 |
| Mindful nudges (gentle friction) | On, adjustable | 1 |
| Virtual pet or plant | On | 2 |
| Swipe budget (optional limit, earn more) | **Off, opt-in** | 2 |
| Focus mode (optional block timer) | **Off, opt-in** | 2 |
| Accountability buddy | **Off, opt-in** | 3 |

## Success measures (first version)
- 7-day retention of 40% or more.
- At least half of active users open Insights once a week.
- Users report noticing their swiping more (in-app one-question survey).

## Non-goals (for now)
- Parental control or managing someone else's phone.
- Analysing what the videos contain.
- Hard blocking that the user cannot turn off.
